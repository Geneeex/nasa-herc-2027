# Data files

The procurement CSVs are row-by-row extracts of the supplied purchase documents, including their original header rows and any optional/spare wording. Column meanings are retained in each `HEADER` row. They are purchasing references, not accepted installed BOMs or newly checked prices.

Use the calculation provenance files to distinguish catalogue data, allowances and the historical installed ledger. Optional parts, stock offcuts and spares must not be included in installed mass. Record actual finished-component masses and quantities in the [mass template](../testing/templates/mass-and-dimensions.csv) before revising the accepted total.

Our current aluminium chassis mass remains unresolved. The source Google Doc and numerical reproduction explain the earlier reported total; they do not establish the current rover's measured mass.
